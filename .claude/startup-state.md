### The wedge — long tail of recurring chapter events (research-confirmed)

National specialists own bid day + composites but **ignore the long tail.** Local wedding pros are priced out. Open market for two-photographer student team:
- Formals + semi-formals
- Swaps + mixers
- Philanthropy nights (Sigma Chi Derby Days, Pi Phi Arrowspike, Alpha Phi King of Hearts, etc.)
- Big/little reveals
- Date functions
- GameDay tailgates
- Sisterhood retreats with activity coverage

**Pricing wedge: $300–$500 flat for 2–3 hr event coverage** with 24–48 hr sneak-peek + 1-week full delivery. Undercuts wedding pros (impossible at MKC's $750 floor), matches Thumbtack's national avg, beats chapter-budget objections.

**Productize three SKUs from day one:** Formal / Philanthropy / Swap, flat fees. Optional chapter-annual retainer (e.g., 6 events for $1,800 saves the chapter ~16%).
